## Multiplicity (robustness)
`robustness.build_robustness_table` → `results/robustness_table.csv`

Pre-specified families: each **primary time-course family = 2 muscles × 4 timepoints
= 8 tests, per PRO instrument** (ODI and Global-PH corrected separately); the
**exploratory texture→MCID family = 3 muscles × 3 instruments = 9 tests**.

| Family | Key result | Raw p | Holm | FDR |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary ΔODI (2 muscles × 4 tp, k=8) | iliopsoas 3 mo | 0.005 | **0.043** | **0.043** |
| Primary ΔODI | iliopsoas 1 y | 0.023 | 0.164 | 0.094 |
| Exploratory texture → MCID (3 muscles × 3 instr., k=9) | iliopsoas ODI-MCID | 0.031 | 0.281 | 0.269 |

> The primary **3-month** ODI effect survives family-wise correction under the
> pre-specified per-instrument family; the **1-year** ODI effect and the exploratory
> ODI-MCID heterogeneity are **nominal only** and do not survive Holm/FDR.
> **Sensitivity:** pooling both co-primary instruments into a single 16-test family
> raises Holm on the 3-mo ODI effect to 0.080 (nominal) while FDR stays 0.043.
> **Fragility:** the ODI-MCID result has a fragility index of 1
> (`results/fragility.csv`); it is treated as hypothesis-generating.
